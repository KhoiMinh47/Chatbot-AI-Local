prompt-only requests invoke Nemotron, uploads are bounded/retryable, retrieval is hybrid,
and citation IDs are stable and validated. The index-version split between legacy SQL chunks
and the active Qdrant contract was repaired for ready documents with migration 0014. A
numerical quality improvement is not reported
because no approved fixed answer-key dataset was available for a baseline/candidate A/B.

Open gates are reranker selection, BF16/NVFP4 A/B, Mamba cache-dtype proof, semantic claim
support, long-document hierarchy, safe true streaming and the full production-like eval
matrix. See `docs/chatbot-quality-progress.md` for the exact status.
